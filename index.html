Mobile apps

Apps used by real people.

Three apps I designed and built end to end: one for a maternity clinic, one for a church, one for expectant mothers.

OM
Android app

OMH Maternity App

Staff app for Oluwatodupefun Maternity Home. An offline-capable Android app plus web dashboard: patient and antenatal records, labour records, a staff duty roster with lateness tracking, and AI-powered drug information and clinical briefs.

App for Mountain of Victory at the Last Day Evangelical Ministry, a Lagos church. Live online radio streaming, a daily Bible quiz with leaderboards, push notifications for church announcements, and secure member login.

BellePal

A Nigeria-first pregnancy companion for expectant mothers. The offline-first core is free forever, built on a 221-topic maternal health knowledge base reviewed with 12 clinicians. The online layer adds AI chat and report scanning.

Built with: Expo, Node.js, Postgres

Process

How I work.

Four steps, no surprises. You always know what happens next.

STEP 01

Discover

We talk through what the site or app needs to do, who it is for, and what success looks like.

STEP 02

Design

I plan the structure, then design every screen: layout, type, color, and content, reviewed with you.

STEP 03

Build

I write the code, test on phones and desktops, and fix what breaks before you ever see it.

STEP 04

Launch

We go live together. I stay around after launch for fixes, updates, and small improvements.
