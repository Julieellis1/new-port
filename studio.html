Who I am.

I’m Adebiyi Thompson, a web designer and app developer based in Lagos, Nigeria. I design and build websites and Android apps for businesses, churches, clinics, and schools, and I work with clients anywhere in the world.

My background is hands-on: I have shipped eleven websites and three mobile apps, from a maternity clinic’s staff app to a church’s online radio app to a pregnancy companion used by expectant mothers. I handle the whole arc: figuring out what to build, designing it, writing the code, and launching it.

Working with me is simple. One person owns the project from start to finish, so feedback goes straight into the work and nothing gets lost between a designer, a developer, and a manager who never met you.

  • ✓Design and code together. The design is built by the person who drew it, so the live site matches the plan.
  • ✓Tested on real phones. Most of your visitors arrive on mobile, so mobile gets designed first, not last.
  • ✓Support after launch. I stay around for fixes, updates, and the small improvements every site needs.
