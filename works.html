Mobile apps

Three apps, three real users.

Designed and built end to end: interface, data, and launch.

OM
Android app

OMH Maternity App

Staff app for Oluwatodupefun Maternity Home. An offline-capable Android app plus web dashboard: patient and antenatal records, labour records, a staff duty roster with lateness tracking, and AI-powered drug information and clinical briefs. The web dashboard is live; ask me for a walkthrough of the Android app.

Built with: Next.js, Postgres, Capacitor

Android app

Movaldem Radio App

App for Mountain of Victory at the Last Day Evangelical Ministry, a Lagos church. Live online radio streaming, a daily Bible quiz with leaderboards, push notifications for church announcements, and secure member login. Ask me for a walkthrough of the Android app.

Built with: native-feel web stack, Firebase push

Mobile app

BellePal

A Nigeria-first pregnancy companion for expectant mothers. The offline-first core is free forever, built on a 221-topic maternal health knowledge base reviewed with 12 clinicians. The online layer adds AI chat and report scanning. Ask me for a walkthrough.

Built with: Expo, Node.js, Postgres
